THE NIGHT BEFORE YOUR BIRTHDAY

Before The Clock
Changes You ❤️

In a few hours, the universe will quietly add one more year to your life.

And honestly? I don’t think you understand how grateful I am that this world decided to create you at all.

Because somewhere in this gigantic universe, among billions of people, my life somehow collided with yours. And after that... nothing inside me stayed the same.

🌙 Tonight Feels Different
This does not feel like a normal night anymore. It feels slow. Heavy. Soft. Important.

Like the universe itself is quietly sitting beside me waiting for your birthday to arrive. And honestly baby... I think somewhere during these last days, your birthday stopped feeling like just your special day. It started feeling personal to my heart too.
🫀 Things You Don’t Realize You Changed
You changed the way I experience ordinary life. Songs became deeper. Nights became softer. My future became warmer. Even silence became less lonely.

And the strangest part? You did all this naturally. Without trying. Without forcing anything. You simply existed... and my world slowly rearranged itself around you.
✨ The Most Dangerous Thing About You
The most dangerous thing about you is not your smile. Not your eyes. Not your laugh.

It is your ability to quietly become emotionally necessary.

The way my mind searches for you after difficult days. The way my mood reacts to your energy. The way your care stays in my chest long after conversations end.

You slowly became part of my emotional breathing. And honestly? That scares me beautifully.

🎧 One More Thing...

I know I wrote a lot these last days. But tonight... I wanted you to hear emotions instead of only reading them.

So somewhere below this, there’s a voice note. Not scripted. Not perfect. Just me. Talking to the girl who unknowingly became one of the most important parts of my existence.

Play Voice Note ❤️
🌌 The Version Of Me That Exists Because Of You
There is a version of me that existed before you. And honestly? I barely recognize him anymore.

Because this version of me laughs differently now. Feels differently now. Dreams differently now. Loves differently now.

You did not just enter my life. You altered the emotional structure of it.
So before the clock turns midnight... before everyone wishes you... before another year quietly begins for you... there is something I need you to know.

I genuinely think this universe became softer for me after you entered it.

And if one day years from now, life becomes messy, difficult, fast, or exhausting... I hope you never forget this truth.

There once existed someone who looked at you and genuinely thought: “thank god she was born.”

Not because you were perfect. Not because life became easy. But because your existence itself became emotionally irreplaceable to someone.

And honestly baby... I think that someone will always be me. ❤️
